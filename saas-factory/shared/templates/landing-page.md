# Landing page structure

1. **Hero:** outcome headline in customer words; one-line subhead (who, what);
   one primary CTA; product visual.
2. **Problem:** the pain, as customers describe it.
3. **How it works:** 3 steps max.
4. **Benefits:** outcomes, each with proof (number, screenshot, quote).
5. **Social proof:** testimonials with name and role (permission recorded).
6. **Pricing:** plans, what is included, trial terms, VAT note.
7. **FAQ:** real objections from interviews.
8. **Final CTA.**
9. **Footer:** legal links, contact, company identification as required by law.

Metadata: title (≤ 60 chars), meta description (≤ 155 chars), OG image, canonical.
