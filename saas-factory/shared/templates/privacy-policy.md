# Privacy notice skeleton (draft; ⚖️ review recommended)

Plain language, layered: short summary first, details after.

1. Who is the controller and how to contact us (and DPO if any)
2. What data we collect and from where
3. Why we use it and the legal basis for each purpose
4. Who we share it with (subprocessors list link) and international transfers
5. How long we keep it
6. Your rights and how to exercise them; right to complain to the authority
7. Cookies and similar technologies
8. Security measures (summary)
9. Changes to this notice; last updated date
