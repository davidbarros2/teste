# Data Processing Agreement skeleton (GDPR Art. 28; ⚖️ review recommended)

1. Parties, roles (customer = controller, we = processor)
2. Subject matter, duration, nature and purpose of processing
3. Types of personal data and categories of data subjects
4. Processor obligations: instructions only, confidentiality, security (Annex),
   assistance with rights and DPIAs, breach notification without undue delay,
   deletion or return at end, audits
5. Subprocessors: list, notice of changes, right to object
6. International transfers and safeguards
7. Annex: technical and organizational measures
