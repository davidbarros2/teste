# Interview script: <product>

Rules: ask about the past and specifics, not opinions about the future. Do not
pitch until the end, if at all. Listen for money, time, workarounds, emotion.

## Opening (2 min)
- Thank them; explain you are researching how <audience> handle <task>.

## Context (5 min)
- Walk me through the last time you <task>. What happened?

## Pain (10 min)
- What was hardest? What did it cost you (time, money, stress)?
- What have you tried to fix it? Why did that not work?
- Do you pay for anything related today? How much?

## Priority (5 min)
- Where does this rank among your problems this quarter?

## Close (3 min)
- Who else should I talk to?
- Can I show you something in a few weeks? (commitment signal)
