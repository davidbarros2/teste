# 07 Pre-launch

**Goal:** legally, operationally and commercially ready to take money in public.

## Inputs
| Source | File | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| Legal | `_config/legal-profile.md`, `legal-framework.md` (+ overrides) | Full |
| Design | `{P}/stages/03_architecture/output/dpia-lite.md` | Data inventory, subprocessors |
| Pricing | `{P}/stages/02_mvp_definition/output/pricing.md` | Full |
| Checklist | `shared/checklists/launch-checklist.md` | Full |
| Skills | `legal-compliance`, `privacy-gdpr`, `landing-pages`, `seo`, `accessibility`, `documentation-support`, `human-writing` | Full |
| Templates | `terms-of-service.md`, `privacy-policy.md`, `dpa.md`, `subprocessors.md`, `accessibility-statement.md` | Full |
| Questions | `references/questions.md` | Full |

## Process
1. Draft legal documents from templates and the product's data inventory.
   Plain language; flag ⚖️ items for professional review.
2. Tax and invoicing checklist per legal-framework (VAT, OSS, invoices).
3. Final landing page copy and structure (audited: copy, SEO, a11y, cookies).
4. Help center: getting started, FAQ, billing, data export and deletion.
5. Operations: alerts, backup restore test, incident runbook, support inbox.
6. Run the launch checklist; list every open item with an owner.

## Outputs
`legal/` (drafts), `tax-invoicing.md`, `landing-final.md`, `help/`,
`runbook.md`, `launch-checklist.md` (filled), `GATE.md`.

## Gate
- [ ] All ⚖️ items reviewed by a professional or consciously accepted
- [ ] Invoices and VAT tested with a real transaction
- [ ] Restore from backup tested this month
- [ ] Launch checklist complete
