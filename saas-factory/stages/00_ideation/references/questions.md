# 00 Ideation questions

## Portfolio mode
1. Generate new ideas, score ideas you already have, or both?
2. Niches or professions to explore first (from founder.md access list)?
3. Any idea seeds: annoyances you see at work, in forums, in your network?

## Product mode
1. Which backlog idea? (show top 3 by score)
2. Who exactly is the buyer, and who is the user (same person?)
3. How do they solve it today, and what does that cost them?
4. Which archetype(s) fit? (propose from archetypes/_index.md)
5. product_language: default en-US, or the niche's language?
6. Riskiest assumption: what, if false, kills the idea?
