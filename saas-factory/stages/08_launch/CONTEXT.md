**Goal:** reach the niche where it already gathers, more than once.

## Process
1. `launch-plan.md`: channels ranked by audience fit, sequence over 2-6 weeks,
   one message per channel, success metrics.
2. Channel-specific copy in `copy/<channel>.md`, native to each channel's norms.
3. Launch-day checklist: monitoring watch, support coverage, rollback plan.
4. ⏸ Checkpoint: founder publishes and engages; results to `results.md`.
5. Retrospective: what worked, cost per signup, next channels.

## Outputs
`launch-plan.md`, `copy/`, `results.md`, `GATE.md` in `output/`.

## Gate
- [ ] Each channel's rules respected (no spam, disclosed affiliation)
- [ ] Results recorded with numbers
- [ ] Lessons appended to `learnings/LEARNINGS.md`
