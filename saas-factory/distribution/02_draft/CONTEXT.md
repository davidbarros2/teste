## Process
1. Brief: format, angle, reader, one takeaway, call to action.
2. Outline, then draft. Lead with the reader's problem, not the product.
3. Use concrete examples, numbers and customer language. Cite sources.
4. Audit with `human-writing` and `copywriting` checklists; fix before summary.
