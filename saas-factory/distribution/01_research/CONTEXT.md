## Process
1. List questions and problems the audience has, in their words.
2. Map each to search intent and to a channel (search, community, newsletter).
3. Score by demand evidence, fit with the product, and effort.
4. Never invent search volumes; mark them unknown unless the founder supplies data.

## Outputs
`output/topics.md`: ranked topics with intent, channel, evidence, status.
