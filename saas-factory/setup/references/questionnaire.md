# Setup questionnaire

Ask per the interaction protocol (3 options, recommendation when possible).

## 1. Founder profile
- Skills: programming level, languages and frameworks you know, design, writing, marketing, sales.
- Weekly hours available for the factory. Fixed days?
- Monthly budget for tools and services.
- Industries, professions or communities you know or can reach (your "unfair access").
- Income goal and time horizon (e.g. 2,000 €/month MRR in 18 months).
- Risk appetite: keep a day job? bootstrap only?

## 2. Legal and tax profile
- Natural person or company? Legal form (self-employed with open activity /
  sole trader / single-member company / Lda / other).
- Country of tax residence and of the business address.
- VAT regime (normal, small-business exemption, other) and accountant (yes/no).
- Where you will sell: Portugal, EU, UK, USA, worldwide.
- To whom: B2B, B2C or both.
- What: SaaS subscriptions, one-off digital products, services, other.
- Payment setup preference: merchant of record (Paddle, Lemon Squeezy) or
  direct processor (Stripe) + certified invoicing software.
- Personal data you expect to process: customer accounts only, or also your
  customers' end-users' data? Any special categories (health, minors, biometrics)?
- Regulated sectors you might target (health, finance, education, legal).

## 3. Legal framework
No questions: draft from block 2 using `legal-compliance` and `privacy-gdpr`
skills. Validate item by item with the founder.

## 4. Idea criteria
- Must-haves (e.g. B2B, recurring pain, reachable audience, no heavy regulation).
- Deal-breakers (e.g. marketplaces with chicken-and-egg problems, hardware).
- Minimum price point and target number of customers.
- Kill criteria for validation (e.g. fewer than 3 strong buying signals in 15 interviews).

## 5. Default stack
- Stack you know best. Hosting preference (EU data residency?).
- Managed services allowed (auth, DB, email, payments, analytics, errors).

## 6. Brand voice and writing style
- Three adjectives for the voice. Brands whose tone you like and dislike.
- Formality, humor, use of "you"/"we".

## 7. Preferences
- English variant (default US). Default `product_language`.
- Fast mode allowed by default? Answer mode preferred (all / one by one)?
