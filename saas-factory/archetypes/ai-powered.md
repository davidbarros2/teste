# Archetype: AI-powered

Combine with another archetype.

## Technical
- Evaluation set from real cases before shipping; track quality per release.
- Cost per user and per action monitored; limits per plan.
- Human-in-the-loop for consequential outputs; show sources when possible.
- Never send personal data to a model provider without a DPA and a legal basis.

## Legal
- AI Act: classify the use case (prohibited, high-risk, limited/transparency, minimal).
  Most micro SaaS are limited or minimal risk; transparency duties still apply
  (e.g. telling users they interact with AI, labeling generated content).
- Model provider is a subprocessor; check data retention and training terms.

## Pricing
- Credits or usage tiers protect margins; avoid unlimited plans.

- `ai-features`.
