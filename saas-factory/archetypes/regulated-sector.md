# Archetype: Regulated sector (overlay)

Combine with another archetype. Raises the bar; consider avoiding for a first product.

## Health
- Health data is a GDPR special category: explicit legal basis, DPIA required,
  stronger security, possibly medical-device rules if the software diagnoses or treats.

## Finance
- Payment services, investment advice and credit are licensed activities; stay
  out of the regulated core or partner with licensed providers.

## Minors
- Parental consent rules, age-appropriate design, stricter profiling limits.

## Process changes
- Professional legal review before stage 02 gate, not only at 07.
- Full DPIA in stage 03. Security testing by a third party before launch.
