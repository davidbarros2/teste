# Archetype: Marketplace plugin

## Technical
- Follow the platform's app guidelines, OAuth scopes and webhooks; handle uninstall cleanly.
- Platform API changes are your main operational risk: monitor changelogs.

## Legal
- Platform partner terms; revenue share; data handling rules of the platform.

## Pricing
- Often billed through the platform; respect its billing rules.

## Distribution
- Marketplace listing, reviews, platform partner programs, community of that platform.
