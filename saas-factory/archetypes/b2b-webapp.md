# Archetype: B2B web app

## Technical
- Team accounts and roles from day one (owner, member); invitations.
- Tenant isolation is the top security risk. Audit logs for admin actions.
- SSO (SAML/OIDC) only when customers ask; often a higher plan.

## Legal
- You are usually a GDPR **processor** for customer data: DPA and subprocessor list required.
- Business customers: reverse-charge VAT inside the EU (valid VAT number check).

## Pricing
- Per seat, per usage or flat tiers. Annual plans with discount. Higher price tolerance than B2C.

## Distribution
- Communities of the profession, LinkedIn, partnerships, SEO on job-to-be-done queries, direct outreach.
