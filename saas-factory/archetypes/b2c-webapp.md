# Archetype: B2C web app

## Technical
- Self-serve everything: sign-up, billing, cancellation, data export and deletion.
- Mobile-first UI; performance matters more.

## Legal
- Consumer law: pre-contract information, 14-day withdrawal rules for digital
  services, Digital Content Directive conformity, clear cancellation.
- Accessibility (European Accessibility Act) likely applies unless micro-enterprise exemption.
- VAT charged at the customer's country rate (OSS). Merchant of record simplifies this.
- Portugal: electronic complaints book and ADR information.

## Pricing
- Lower price, higher churn; monthly + annual; trials or freemium common.

## Distribution
- Content and SEO, social platforms, communities, app directories. Cost per acquisition is critical.
