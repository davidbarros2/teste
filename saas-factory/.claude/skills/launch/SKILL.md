# Launch

Last reviewed: 2026-10.

## Rules
1. Launch is a sequence, not a day: waitlist and beta first, then niche
   communities, directories, partners, broader platforms.
2. Warm your audience before launch (build in public, waitlist updates).
3. One message per channel, native to its format and norms.
4. Offer with a real reason and a real deadline (founder pricing), never fake scarcity.
5. Be present: reply fast to every comment and support request on launch days.
6. Operations ready: monitoring, support coverage, rollback plan, capacity check.
7. Measure per channel: visits, sign-ups, activations, paid. Retro after.

## Audit checklist
- [ ] Sequence planned with dates and owners
- [ ] Each channel's rules checked
- [ ] Launch-day checklist done
- [ ] Results and lessons recorded
