# Customer research

Last reviewed: 2026-10.

## Problem framing
"<audience> loses <time/money> doing <task> because <cause>". Start from
people you can reach, never from a technology.

## Interview rules (The Mom Test)
1. Talk about their life and past behavior, not your idea.
2. Ask about specifics ("the last time..."), not generics or the future.
3. Talk less, listen more. Never pitch during discovery.
4. Compliments are noise; facts, money and commitments are signal.
5. Every meeting should end with a commitment: time, intro, or money.

## Synthesis
- Tag notes: pains, workarounds, spend, triggers, objections, quotes.
- Rank pains by frequency × intensity. Keep verbatim language.
- Separate what you heard from what you infer.

## Smoke tests
Landing page with price and CTA (waitlist or pre-sale). Measure visitors →
signups → payments. Pre-payment is the strongest signal.

## Audit checklist
- [ ] Questions are about past behavior; no leading questions
- [ ] Conclusions cite notes; inferences labeled as such
- [ ] Buying signals counted by type (compliment, time, intro, money)
