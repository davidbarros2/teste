# Landing pages

Last reviewed: 2026-10. Template: `shared/templates/landing-page.md`.

## Rules
1. Above the fold: outcome headline, who it is for, primary CTA, product visual.
2. Show the product. Real screenshots or a short demo beat illustrations.
3. Social proof near decisions (hero, pricing, final CTA). Real names and roles.
4. Pricing visible and honest: VAT treatment, renewal terms, trial terms.
5. FAQ built from real objections.
6. Fast (Core Web Vitals good), accessible (WCAG 2.2 AA), mobile-first.
7. Legal footer: company identification, terms, privacy, cookies.
8. Measure: one conversion event per CTA; test one change at a time.
9. No dark patterns: no fake scarcity, hidden costs, confirmshaming,
   pre-ticked boxes.

## Audit checklist
- [ ] 5-second test passes (what, who, why, next step)
- [ ] One primary CTA repeated, consistent wording
- [ ] Proof for each key claim
- [ ] Pricing, taxes and trial terms clear
- [ ] Skills `copywriting`, `seo`, `accessibility`, `web-performance`, `human-writing` audits pass
- [ ] Cookie handling per `privacy-gdpr`
