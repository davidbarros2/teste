# Storytelling and positioning

Last reviewed: 2026-10.

## Positioning (in order)
1. Competitive alternatives: what customers would use if you did not exist
   (often spreadsheets, an agency, doing nothing).
2. Unique attributes you have that alternatives lack.
3. Value those attributes enable, with proof.
4. Who cares most about that value (best-fit customers).
5. Market category that makes the value obvious.

## Narrative
- The customer is the hero; the product is the guide.
- Structure: hero with a problem → meets a guide with a plan → takes action →
  avoids failure → reaches success.
- Use real stories from interviews and beta users (with permission).

## Jobs to be done
- Describe the progress the customer is trying to make in a situation,
  including functional, emotional and social dimensions.

## Audit checklist
- [ ] Alternatives named from evidence, not assumed
- [ ] Differentiator is true, provable and matters to best-fit customers
- [ ] One-liner: "<Product> helps <who> <achieve outcome> without <pain>"
- [ ] Story centers the customer, not the founder or the tech
