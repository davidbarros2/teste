# Content and distribution

Last reviewed: 2026-10.

## Rules
1. Go where the niche already gathers; rank channels by audience fit and
   your unfair access, then test 2-3 cheaply (bullseye method).
2. Give value first: answer questions, share templates, tools, data.
3. Respect each community's rules; disclose that you build the product.
4. Content from evidence: real customer questions, real numbers, first-hand
   experience. One clear takeaway per piece.
5. Repurpose: one core piece → posts, newsletter, short video, docs.
6. Track source of sign-ups (UTM or "how did you hear about us").
7. Partnerships and integrations with tools the niche already uses.
8. Endorsements and affiliates must be disclosed.

## Channel norms (examples)
- Reddit/forums: participate before posting; no link-only posts.
- LinkedIn: personal profiles outperform pages; native text over links.
- Newsletters: consented lists only.

## Audit checklist
- [ ] Each piece tied to a researched topic and a channel
- [ ] Disclosure present where required
- [ ] Results recorded and reviewed weekly
